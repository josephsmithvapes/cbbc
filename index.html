· EIGHT COMMITMENTS ·

We are starting a coffee company in public. This page lists what we have promised — to each other and to you — and the brew rig below is the receipt.

WHAT WE WILL DO — AND NOT DO

Eight commitments. Hold us to them.

Every coffee we make goes through an instrumented brew rig. Temperature, weight, time, agitation — recorded automatically. No batch is exempt.
Readings post to this page sub-second. While we're brewing, you can watch. While we're not, the archive is here for you to read.
Including the brews that fail. Including the ones we dump. Especially those. A transparency claim that excludes failures is a marketing claim.
Once a reading is published it cannot be revised. Errata get appended below the original, signed and dated.
We are not asking for your money. There is no store. There is no subscription. When that changes, you will hear it from us first — by email.
Bootstrapped on purpose. If we eventually take outside capital, the terms will be published on this same page.
Cris Zelaya runs the process and the IoT. Sky Madsen runs palate and sourcing. If a decision was made, one of us made it. We will sign our names to it.
If the marketing says one thing and the sensor log says another, the sensor log wins. Every time.

The Temperature Drop BATCH 015 · BLEND TRIAL A
MAY 13 · 2026
· DISPATCH ·

Today's steep ran four degrees colder than planned. Not a failure — the data revealed something. At 34°F the extraction curve flattened almost completely by hour 16, producing a body that reads rounder on the back palate than our previous trial.

We're keeping this batch. Sky flagged it as the closest so far to what we're chasing — a sweetness without acidity that doesn't taste manipulated. The numbers back it up.

The grinder was running too coarse on the first attempt. Twelve minutes in, we re-calibrated mid-session — something we won't be able to do at scale, but at 2-quart volume, the adjustment was clean. The extraction finished uniform.

Temperature held steady at 38.2°F through the full 20 hours. No deviations. This batch becomes our first clean baseline.

Day one. Rig online, sensors calibrated, first grind measured. The fridge temperature settled higher than target — 39.0°F versus the 36°F we're eventually chasing — but we ran it anyway. First batch, first log, first number that is real.

The result was thin. Expected. We needed a starting point, not a product. This is the starting point.
