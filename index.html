01 · The Process

THE PROCESS IS THE PRODUCT.

STEP 01
GRIND

Coarse ground, fresh every single batch. Never pre-ground, never stored.

Fresh · Coarse · Every Batch
STEP 02
STEEP

Cold water immersion for exactly 20 hours. No heat. No shortcuts. Slow by design.

Cold · 20 Hours · No Heat
STEP 03
SERVE

Over ice. Bold, never bitter. A cup you can trace back to the exact batch it came from.

Bold · Over Ice · Traceable
Live Brew Monitor · IoT

An ESP32-powered sensor streams real-time batch status to every customer — Grinding → Steeping → Ready. The first cold brew you can actually watch being made.
